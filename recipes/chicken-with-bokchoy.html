Chicken Mince and Bok Choy Stir-Fry

Credits to

Description

Asian style chicken with vegetables, healthy dinner option for all.

Prep time: 10 minutes, Cook time: 10-12 minutes, Serving: 1

Ingredients

  • 130-160g chicken mince
  • 2-3 baby bok choy, halved or quartered lengthwise, leaves and stems separated
  • 1.2 Cup sliced capsicum
  • 1/2 Cup Snap Peace, trimmed
  • 1 Cup finely shredded cabbage
  • 1 Tsp extra virgin oilive oil or sesame oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 Tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1-2 Tsp soy sauce, plus splash of water or lemon juice

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large skillet or wok over medium high heat. Add the chicken mince, breaking it up with a spatula, and cook for 5-6 minutes until browned and cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
  2. In the same pan over medium high heat, add the minced garlic, grated ginger, shredded cabbage, capsicum, snap peas, and the stems of the bok choy. Stir-fry for 2-3 minutes until slightly tender.
  3. Add the bok choy leaves and return the cooked mince to the pan.
  4. Add soy sauce and 2 table spoon of water if needed to create a light steam. Toss for 1-2 minutes until the greens wilt and everything is hot, then serve immediately.

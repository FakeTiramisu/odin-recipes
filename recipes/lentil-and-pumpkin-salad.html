Lentil and Roasted Pumpkin Salad

Image credits to fresh choice

Description

A lunch recipe that can be meal prepped to take with you for work or school.

Prep time: 10 minutes, Cook time: 15-18 minutes, Serving: 1

Ingredients

  • 1/2 to 3/4 Cup pre-cooked brown or green lentils (80-130g)
  • 1 Cup cubed pumpkin
  • 1-1.5 Cups baby spinach or arugula/rocket leaves
  • 25-30g crumbled feta cheese or dollops of yogurt
  • 1 Tsp balsamic vinegar
  • Pinch of black pepper, dried herbs, and light pinch of salt (balance with saltiness of feta)

Steps

  1. Prepheat oven to 200°C. Toss the cubed pumpkin with a light drizzle of olive oil, black pepper, and optional herbs on a lined backing tray, and roast for 15-18 minutes until tender and lightly caramelized at the edges.
  2. Warm the pre-cooked lentils gently in a small pan or microwave for 1-2 minutes until just heated through.
  3. Place the baby spinach in a bowl, then top with the warm lentils and roasted pumpkin so the greens wilt slightly without turning soggy.
  4. Sprinkle the crumbled feta over the top and drizzle with olive oil, balsamic glaze and lemon juice (all 1 Tsp) before serving

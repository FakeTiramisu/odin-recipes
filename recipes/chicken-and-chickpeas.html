Chicken and Chickpea Salad with Greens

Image credits to

Description

Chickpeas made delicious! Extra fiber and flavour. Note that chicken can be replaced with other meat like canned tuna.

Prep time: 8 minutes, No cook time!, Serving: 1

Ingredients

  • 120-140g of chicken breast, cooked (prepped beforehand)
  • 1/2 Cup Cooked Chickpeas, rinsed well
  • 1/2 Cup finely diced celery
  • 2 Tbsp diced red onion
  • 1-1.5 Cups Baby Spinach (or/and rocket leaves)
  • 1 Tbsp fresh lemon juice or apple cider vinegar
  • 1 Tsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Pinch of black pepper and dried dill or parsley

Steps

  1. Heat up the chicken and cut it into edible size. Rinse chickpeas under cold water, then drain well
  2. In medium bowl, combine chicken, chickpeas, diced celery and red onion
  3. Stir in lemon juice, evoo, black pepper, and herbs
  4. Spoon the tuna and chickpea mixture over the baby spinach in a bowl and toss gently before eating

Grilled White Fish with Cauliflower Mash

Image credits to Clean Eating

Description

Cauliflower and white fish is a great combination. Have some faith in cauliflower. White fish fillet is used in this recipe, this can be barramundi, cod or ling.

Prep time: 10 minutes, Cook time: 15-18 minutes, Serving: 1

Ingredients

  • 140-170g white fish fillet
  • 1.5 Cups cauloflower florets
  • 1.5-2 Cups chopped baby spinach
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • Pinch of salt, black pepper and parika or dried dill
  • 1 Tbsp fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Place cauliflower florets in a steamer basket over simmering water for 8-10 minutes until fork-tender. Drain thoroughly and mash with a fork or masher. Mix in splash of lemon juice, black pepper, and a small touch of olive oil.
  2. Pat the fish dry with a paper towel and season lightly with salt, black pepper, and optional paprika or dill. Cook in a skillet (non-stick) over medium heat with 1/2 tsp oil. Cook for 3-4 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until opaque and flaky.
  3. After cooking the fish, heat up the same pan with little bit of olive oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic and cook for 20-30 seconds until fragrant, then add spinach and toss for 2-4 minutes until wilted and tender.
  4. Spoon cauliflower mash onto your plate alongside the white fish and wilted greens. Add a last squeeze of fresh lemon juice.
